Hire Gary

Onboarding takes one line.

Add him with one script tag, make him say your things, or swap him for a character of your own.

  1. Copy the script tag. 
    <script defer src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/gary.js@0.1.0/dist/gary.js"></script>
  2. Paste it into your page, in the <head> or just before </body>. No build step, no account, no backend.
  3. Reload. Gary walks in once your page has loaded and starts having opinions. Pick a preset with one more attribute, or write his lines.

Annoying on purpose. Polite by design.

  • One script tagNo build step, no framework, no backend, no account.
  • SmallAbout 26 KB gzipped. Artwork loads after your page has painted, with no layout shift.
  • Never in the wayClicks pass through every transparent pixel. Your links, forms and buttons keep working.
  • Visitors are in chargeThey can drag him, mute him or send him home, by mouse, keyboard or touch.
  • No trackingNo analytics, no cookies, no network calls beyond his own images.
  • Easy on batteriesStops in background tabs and rests when nobody's around. Respects reduced motion.

Make him say your things

Write your own lines and decide when he says them: on load, on hover, on click, when something scrolls into view, when the visitor goes quiet. Scenes walk him onto your headings and buttons.

Configuration reference →

<script type="application/json" data-gary-config>
{
  "schemaVersion": 1,
  "lines": {
    "pricing": ["Pricing? Let's set up a call
                 to discuss a call."]
  },
  "triggers": [
    { "on": "click", "selector": "#contact",
      "say": "#pricing" },
    { "on": "idle", "after": 30,
      "say": "Still there? Bold." }
  ]
}
</script>

Don't like Gary? Replace him.

The engine knows nothing about Gary. Describe any character in a JSON manifest with one image per animation, and it walks, talks and gets dragged around just the same. Mugsy, the public-domain mug, is a complete example to start from.
