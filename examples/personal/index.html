About

I'm a founder and operator with fifteen years in B2B sales. I believe the best sales teams are led by people who combine a visionary product sense with the discipline to follow up on every lead.

I started my career as a sales rep, ran revenue at two growth-stage startups, and founded Cartwheel Analytics, acquired in 2021. In 2023 I started Leveragely to give every rep an assistant that writes, follows up and keeps the CRM up to date.
