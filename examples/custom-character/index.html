Meet Mugsy

A custom character running on the same engine. Mugsy is public-domain sample art made for this repository.

<script defer src="gary.core.js"
        data-character="https://your.cdn/mugsy/character.json"></script>

Mugsy only has a walk-right clip and marks it "mirror": true, so the engine flips it to walk left. Mugsy has no pointer-inspection or idle-variant clip; those parts of the behaviours are simply skipped.
