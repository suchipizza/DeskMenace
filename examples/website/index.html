Frequently asked questions

Can't find an answer? Talk to our team.

Which email providers do you support?

Gmail and Microsoft Outlook, including shared inboxes on the Growth plan.

Is my data used to train models?

No. Your emails and notes are only used to generate drafts for your team.

Do emails go out without review?

Only if you turn on Autopilot for a sequence. Otherwise every draft waits for a rep's approval.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled from your billing settings.
