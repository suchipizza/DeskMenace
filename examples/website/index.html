Seven productivity tips nobody asked for

A short guide to getting more done, from people who wrote it during a meeting.

1. Write things down

Lists are great. Lists of lists are better. A list of the lists you've lost is a lifestyle.

2. Block your calendar

Put "focus time" in your calendar. Then accept the meeting someone schedules over it.

3. Take breaks

Studies show breaks improve focus. The studies were read during a break.

4. Say no

Practise saying no to things. Start with this article.

5. Single-task

Do one thing at a time. Ideally the thing you were asked to do.

6. Automate

If you do something three times, automate it. If the automation breaks three times, do it by hand.

7. Rest

Sleep is the original productivity tool. It has no subscription.
